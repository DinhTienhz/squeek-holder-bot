How it works

Simple, transparent, and secure trading on-chain

📋

List

Approve the escrow and set a price. The Squeak cash inside the contract until it sells or you pull it back.

🛒

Buy

The buyer pays the price plus the 0.1% fee; the NFT and the ETH move in the same transaction.

💰

Bid

Lock ETH at any price for the whole collection. The earliest bid at a level is served first, and any holder can accept — the seller pays the 0.1%.

↩️

Cancel anytime

Sellers can withdraw a listed Squeak. Bidders can cancel a bid and get their ETH back.
