Collections

NFT collections you can buy and sell here. Each one settles through its own escrow.
1

List

Approve the escrow and set a price. The Squeek waits inside the contract until it sells or you pull it back.

2

Buy

The buyer pays the price plus the 0.1% fee; the NFT and the ETH move in the same transaction.

3

Bid

Lock ETH at any price for the whole collection. The earliest bid at a level is served first, and any holder can accept — the seller pays the 0.1%.

4

Cancel anytime

Sellers can withdraw a listed Squeek; bidders can cancel a bid and get their ETH back.
