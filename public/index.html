Eight things it does

Built on the public Squeekchain API and the Squeek contracts.

1 Claim alerts

Get a DM when unclaimed SQK crosses your threshold, then only when it doubles or after a few hours.

2 Claim optimizer

/claim packs the best 32 tokens into one batch and shows the dust left behind.

3 Cooldowns

Know the moment a Squeek leaves cooldown, plus /mergecheck for the full list.

4 Merge ladder

/ladder turns your holdings into an ordered merge plan with the power gain.

5 Hamster alerts

A DM when the wheel starts or stops, with live RPM. Toggle with /hamster.

6 Block alerts

Every completed Squeek block, with the SQK it minted.

7 Wallet activity

Every transfer, merge, claim and rewards claim on your wallets, with an Etherscan link. Toggle with /activity.

8 Quiet hours + digest

Silence at night, one clean summary at the hour you choose.

How it works

Three steps, no wallet connection needed for alerts.

Find the bot by its Telegram username and press /start. It is public — no invite required.

Add a wallet with /watch 0x… label. The bot only reads public chain data for that address.

Get pinged when something matters. To act, the bot links you to the official Squeekchain app — signing always happens in your own wallet.

🧪
More features are in development — shipping soon. Follow @SqueekHolder_Bot to get them first.

Security

The bot never asks for a seed phrase or private key and cannot sign transactions. It stores only your Telegram ID, the wallet addresses you add, and your alert settings. /delete wipes everything.
